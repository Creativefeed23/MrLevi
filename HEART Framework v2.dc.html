The HEART™ Framework

It's all about HEART.

Home is where every journey begins. Love is how every journey is lived. Legacy is what remains.

A framework for every stage

At Mr Levi's Legacy™, everything is all about HEART. The HEART™ Companion Animal Care Framework recognises that caring for a companion animal is not a series of isolated events. It is a lifelong relationship built on connection, trust, advocacy, compassion and love.

Whether your companion is young and thriving, growing older, living with illness, approaching the end of life, or remembered after they have gone, the HEART™ Framework provides a compassionate guide for every stage of the journey.

It reminds us that good care is about more than treating illness. It is also about nurturing wellbeing, honouring relationships, supporting informed decisions, and creating meaningful memories along the way.

HEART is made up of five domains - five HEART Hubs.

Whatever you're looking for, whether an article, a guide, a resource or a product, it has a home under one of these five letters. Explore each below.

H Home and Belonging Start here. You're not alone. E Enrichment & Living Well Learn and feel prepared. A Ageing, Adaptation & Advocacy Walk the journey together. R Reflection, Remembrance, Returning Honour their unique life. T Together, Through Every Stage Keep their love close always.
H

Home & Belonging

A place where love begins.

The foundation of everything. Home is not simply a place, it is the relationship between people and their companion animals. It is where trust develops, where safety is felt, where routines create security, and where love grows. This Hub is about creating an environment where both companion animals and their humans experience connection, comfort, belonging and emotional safety throughout life's journey.

Includes the human-animal bond emotional wellbeing safe environments daily routines enrichment understanding behaviour building trust preparing for life's unexpected changes

E

Enrichment & Living Well

Helping every companion thrive.

Supporting the best life possible. Living well means more than surviving, it means supporting and helping companion animals experience joy, comfort, purpose, play, movement and connection, according to their individual needs and abilities. This Hub promotes physical, emotional, cognitive and social wellbeing across every stage of life.

Includes nutrition exercise mental stimulation preventive healthcare training grooming quality of life age-appropriate enrichment

A

Ageing, Adaptation & Advocacy

Supporting changing needs with compassion.

Growing older, living with illness, adapting with compassion. Ageing is not a problem to be fixed, it is a natural stage of life's journey that deserves understanding, patience and thoughtful adaptation. Illness, injury, disability and changing health needs can arise at any stage of life. This Hub is about noticing change early, adapting care, partnering with veterinary professionals, advocating for comfort, and making informed decisions that support quality of life, whether the need is temporary, chronic or life-limiting.

Includes healthy ageing senior care acute illness chronic disease injury recovery disability support cognitive decline mobility changes home adaptations veterinary partnerships shared decision-making advance care planning quality-of-life discussions

R

Reflection, Remembrance & Returning

Honouring every chapter.

Finding meaning throughout the journey. Reflection encourages us to pause, notice, celebrate and learn throughout our companion's life, not only after loss. Remembrance honours the love, experiences and memories that shape us. And Returning reminds us that love continues through memories, rituals, storytelling, community, acts of kindness and, when the time feels right, opening our hearts again. This Hub embraces grief as a natural expression of love, while recognising that every relationship leaves a legacy that continues to influence our lives.

Includes reflection gratitude meaning-making legacy projects remembrance memorial rituals continuing bonds storytelling grief support returning to hope, community and love
Explore the Reflection, Remembrance & Returning Hub →
T

Together, Through Every Stage

Walking beside every family.

No journey is ever identical. Some companions live long, healthy lives; others experience illness, surgery, disability, palliative care, hospice support or unexpected loss. The HEART™ Framework recognises that compassionate care extends across every stage of life's journey. Together, through education, practical resources, advocacy, community and collaboration with veterinary professionals we support companion animals and the people who love them, from the first day of belonging through to remembrance and legacy.

Includes preventive care hospitalisation support recovery support palliative care hospice care comfort care end-of-life care family support veterinary partnerships community support education resources advocacy
