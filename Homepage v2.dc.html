It's all about HEART

A gentle place for anyone who loves a companion animal.

Whether you're welcoming a new arrival, savouring the good years, caring for a changing or ageing friend, facing difficult days, or holding a beloved memory close there is something here for you.

Everything we create grows from one simple belief: that caring for a companion animal is not a series of isolated moments, but a lifelong relationship that deserves compassion at every stage. That belief has a name, the HEART™ Companion Animal Care Framework and it is the heart of all we do.

It began with one small dog named Levi, who quietly became my greatest teacher. This is his legacy and a place we hope will become a trusted companion to you, wherever your journey leads.
